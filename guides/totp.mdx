TOTP (Time-based One-Time Password) two-factor authentication adds a second layer of security to your RAPID Ledger account. After entering your password, you'll be prompted for a 6-digit code generated by an authenticator app on your phone. Even if your password is compromised, an attacker cannot access your account without that code. RAPID Ledger supports any standard TOTP app, including **Google Authenticator** and **Authy**.

## Setting Up TOTP

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Account Settings">
    Log in to RAPID Ledger and navigate to **Account Settings** from the top-right user menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable Two-Factor Authentication">
    Locate the **Security** section and click **Enable Two-Factor Authentication**. RAPID Ledger will generate a unique TOTP secret for your account and display it as a QR code.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scan the QR Code">
    Open Google Authenticator or Authy on your mobile device and scan the QR code displayed on screen. Your authenticator app will add a new entry labelled **RAPID Ledger** and begin generating 6-digit codes that rotate every 30 seconds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm Setup">
    Enter the current 6-digit code from your authenticator app into the confirmation field in RAPID Ledger and click **Verify**. Once verified, TOTP is active on your account. Save the backup codes displayed on the next screen in a secure location.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Save your backup codes when you set up TOTP. If you lose access to your authenticator app **and** your backup codes, you will be locked out of your account and will need to contact your organisation Admin to reset your TOTP configuration.
</Warning>

## Signing In with TOTP

Once TOTP is enabled, signing in becomes a two-step process.

**Step 1 — Submit your email and password**

Send a `POST` request to `/api/auth/login` with your credentials. If your account has TOTP enabled, the response will not return a session token yet. Instead, it returns a flag indicating that MFA is required along with your `userId`.

```bash
curl -X POST https://rapid-ledger-production.up.railway.app/api/auth/login \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "email": "alice@company.com",
    "password": "your-password"
  }'
```

```json
{
  "requiresMfa": true,
  "userId": "clx4f9z0000001a2b3c4d5e6f"
}
```

**Step 2 — Validate your TOTP code**

Open your authenticator app, copy the current 6-digit code, and send it along with the `userId` from Step 1 to `/api/auth/totp/validate`. On success, RAPID Ledger returns your JWT session token.

```bash
curl -X POST https://rapid-ledger-production.up.railway.app/api/auth/totp/validate \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "userId": "clx4f9z0000001a2b3c4d5e6f",
    "code": "847291"
  }'
```

```json
{
  "token": "eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9...",
  "user": {
    "id": "clx4f9z0000001a2b3c4d5e6f",
    "name": "Alice Chen",
    "email": "alice@company.com",
    "role": "creator"
  }
}
```

Store the returned `token` and include it as a `Bearer` token in the `Authorization` header of all subsequent API requests.
