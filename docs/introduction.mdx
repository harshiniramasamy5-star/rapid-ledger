RAPID Ledger is a full-stack compliance platform that enforces the RAPID decision-making framework across your organisation. Every decision is tracked, versioned, and immutably logged from proposal to execution.

## What is RAPID?

The RAPID framework eliminates decision ambiguity by assigning exactly one role per person per document:

| Letter | Role | Responsibility |
|--------|------|----------------|
| R | Recommend | Proposes the decision with context and evidence |
| A | Agree | High-risk approver who signs off before finalisation |
| P | Perform | Executes the approved decision |
| I | Input | Provides expert information (no vote) |
| D | Decide | Admin with final authority — exactly one per document |

## Key features

- **Immutable audit log** — every action is permanently recorded
- **Role-based access** — 6 system roles enforced at the middleware layer
- **Document versioning** — full history preserved on every change
- **PDF export** — compliance-ready export for finalised documents
- **Notion sync** — approved docs push automatically to Notion
- **Account lockout** — 5 failed logins triggers PostgreSQL-backed lockout
