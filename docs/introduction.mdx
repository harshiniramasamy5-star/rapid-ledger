RAPID Ledger is a full-stack compliance platform that enforces the RAPID decision-making framework across your organisation. Every decision is tracked, versioned, and immutably logged from proposal through execution — and automatically archived to Notion.

## What is RAPID?

The RAPID framework eliminates decision ambiguity by assigning exactly one role per person per document:

| Letter | Role | Responsibility |
|--------|------|----------------|
| R | Recommend | Proposes the decision with context and evidence |
| A | Agree | High-risk approver who signs off before finalisation |
| P | Perform | Executes the approved decision |
| I | Input | Provides expert information — no vote |
| D | Decide | Admin with final authority — exactly one per document |

## Key Features

- **Immutable audit log** — every action permanently recorded, tamper-proof
- **Role-based access control** — 6 system roles enforced at the middleware layer
- **Document versioning** — full history preserved on every change
- **Transcript pipeline** — paste Fathom Video transcripts, linked as evidence
- **Notion sync** — approved docs push automatically to your Notion database
- **TOTP 2FA** — Google Authenticator / Authy support
- **Organisation multi-tenancy** — full org isolation, invite flow, audit per org
- **PDF export** — compliance-ready export for finalised documents
